01 / About

About the ADPCG

The Advanced Data Products Coordination Group (ADPCG) is an open, international initiative bringing together radio observatories, researchers, software developers, and the broader scientific community.

The group aims to foster collaboration, share expertise and best practices, and promote the development of interoperable, sustainable, and scientifically valuable Advanced Data Products (ADPs).

By encouraging coordination between observatories and the community, the ADPCG seeks to maximize the scientific return of radio astronomy data and facilitate broader access to science-ready products.

02 / Priorities

Areas of focus

Collaboration

Connecting observatories, researchers, and developers to share expertise and coordinate initiatives.

Standards & interoperability

Promoting common approaches to data formats, metadata, provenance, and interoperability.

Best practices

Exchanging experience in data processing, quality assurance, and scientific workflows.

Sustainability

Encouraging scalable, efficient, and sustainable approaches to developing and maintaining ADPs.
